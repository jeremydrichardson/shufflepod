How to Use in Overcast

  1. Enter your podcast feed URL above and click "Generate Feed URL"
  2. Copy the generated URL
  3. Open Overcast app on your device
  4. Tap the "+" button to add a podcast
  5. Select "Add URL" and paste your shuffled feed URL
  6. Tap "Add" and enjoy your shuffled podcast!

How It Works

ShufflePod dynamically generates shuffled versions of podcast feeds:

  • Dynamic: Feed is generated fresh each time Overcast checks for updates
  • Consistent: Using the same seed always produces the same shuffle order
  • Up-to-date: New episodes from the original feed are automatically included
  • Cached: Overcast caches the feed, so we're not constantly fetching

Example URLs

Your generated feed URL will look like:

https://yoursite.netlify.app/api/feed?url=https://example.com/podcast.xml&seed=12345

Without seed (random shuffle based on timestamp):

https://yoursite.netlify.app/api/feed?url=https://example.com/podcast.xml
